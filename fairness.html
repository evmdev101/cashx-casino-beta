How Dice RNG Works

Dice uses a commit and reveal flow. Before placing the bet, the site creates a random secret in the browser and sends only a hash of that secret to the contract. After the next block is available, the player reveals the secret and the contract combines it with future block data to create the roll.

Plain-English Flow

  • The player submits a hidden commitment before the result block exists.
  • The contract stores the bet and sets the target block to the next block.
  • The player reveals the secret after the target block is mined.
  • The roll is generated from the target blockhash, player address, secret, bet id, and contract address.
This is stronger than using only block.timestamp or the current block, but a block producer may still have some influence. For that reason, this beta version should not be described as VRF or fully provably fair.

How Multiplayer RNG Works

Multiplayer games wait until the lobby fills, then the contract sets settlement to a future block. After that block exists, anyone can settle the game. The result is generated from the settlement blockhash and game data.

Why Settlement Is Separate

  • Players cannot know the settlement blockhash when the lobby is still filling.
  • The game does not resolve in the same transaction as the final join.
  • If a game is not settled in time, the contract includes an expiry refund path.

Fees & Burns

Dice

Every settled dice bet burns 3%. Winning bets pay 1.95x total. Losing bet leftovers stay in the contract reserve so the game can keep paying future winners.

Card War

Players fund the pot. The winner receives 95% of the completed pot and 5% is burned forever.

Live Mines

Every settled mines game burns 3%. If the player hits a mine, the remaining bet stays in the contract reserve. If the player cashes out, payout comes from the reserve.
