Game Funding & Payouts

CASHX Casino games use funded on-chain contracts to pay winners. The game pages do not display live prize-pool balances because those numbers can be misleading in a casino UI: a high balance can look like a jackpot, and a low balance can look alarming even when payout limits and reserves are working as designed.

VS House games are funded from their contract reserves and include payout caps, bet limits, and burn mechanics. Multiplayer games are funded by the players in each completed pot. Public contract addresses are listed here for anyone who wants to inspect balances and transactions directly on PulseScan.

The simple player-facing rule is: games are funded on-chain, results are verifiable, and CASHX burns are shown publicly. Detailed liquidity checks belong on transparency pages and block explorers, not on the game cards.
